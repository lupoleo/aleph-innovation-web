# Environmental Monitoring

Environmental Monitoring is Aleph Innovation's second R&D project, currently
at concept stage.

Gozo is growing: more visitors, more construction and more traffic on an island
of 67 km². The project asks a simple question: can growth be measured against
the island's environmental limits, in near real time, and in a way anyone can
check?

The project is not designed around the assumption that an LLM is the
application. Sensors, data contracts and deterministic rules carry the
measurements; probabilistic AI assembles evidence inside explicit boundaries;
and a named person makes every public decision.

## What the project explores

- low-cost sensing on land, in sewers and at sea;
- typed data contracts and quality checks for every reading;
- seasonal baselines and EU legal limits as deterministic rules;
- bounded AI agents that assemble and cite evidence;
- human approval for every public advisory;
- an interactive public map of Gozo, in English and Maltese.

## Where it starts

The first phase focuses on the water cycle, where the risks to health and to
the coast are highest:

- sewage overflows and sea outfalls near bathing waters;
- slurry and industrial discharges into valleys and the sea;
- flash floods in the Marsalforn, Xlendi and Għasri valleys.

Later phases add air quality, heat and beach crowding.

## Engineering principles

1. **Deterministic where certainty is possible.**
2. **Probabilistic where interpretation creates value.**
3. **Every estimate carries a confidence and its evidence.**
4. **Every claim is corroborated by two or more independent records.**
5. **Fail-closed behaviour when data is missing or invalid.**
6. **No public advisory without a named person's approval.**
7. **Local where practical: data and open-weight models run in Gozo.**
8. **Architecture, data and validation results are published as the system evolves.**
